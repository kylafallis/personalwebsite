FairGame Initiative

FairGame is a nonprofit organization I founded in November 2025 to close the resource and opportunity gap in STEM education, starting with the science fair pipeline that changed my own trajectory.

We've reached more than 1,000 students, most of them entering their first science fair, through outreach across 10+ school fairs, a statewide teacher network, and a free 288-resource library for teachers running fairs without a budget. The work connects underserved students with the mentorship, funding guidance, and institutional access that too often determines who gets to compete at the national level.

  • Reached 1,000+ students, most entering a science fair for the first time
  • Published a free library of 288 resources for teachers running fairs without a budget
  • Directed outreach across 10+ school science fairs in Ohio
  • Built a statewide network of teachers and mentors supporting first-time competitors
  • Oversees nonprofit finances, legal compliance, and organizational strategy

Co-Founder & CTO · 2024 – Present
Startup · Anaerobic Digestion

EvE Waste

EvE Waste is a startup I co-founded in 2024. Food manufacturers pay to have organic waste hauled off-site and buried, which is a standing cost for them and a methane problem for everyone else.

We build modular anaerobic reactors that sit on the manufacturer's own property, digest that waste where it is produced, and turn the digestate into a nutrient additive we sell on. As CTO I lead reactor design, the digestion process, and how a single module scales into a system.

  • Co-founded the company in 2024 and lead its technical direction as CTO
  • Designs modular anaerobic reactors that deploy on-site at food manufacturers
  • Diverts organic waste from landfill at the source, before it is ever hauled
  • Pilot running at Clocktower, with testing underway on Ohio State's campus
  • Turns digestate into a saleable nutrient additive, so the waste stream pays for itself
