Short Bio · 50 words

Medium Bio · 120 words

Kyla Fallis is a Chemical Engineering student at The Ohio State University's Humanitarian Engineering Scholars Program, pursuing a pre-law minor in Science and Engineering in the Public Interest. She is a published researcher with work appearing in IEEE, Springer, and Sustentabilis Terra, spanning dark oxygen production in the deep sea, physics-informed deep learning for tidal energy, and compost-powered renewable energy systems. In 2026 she worked at NASA's Marshall Space Flight Center in the Electromagnetic Environments Testing Branch.

Kyla is co-founder and CTO of EvE Waste, a startup building modular anaerobic reactors that digest food manufacturing waste on-site and sell the digestate as a nutrient additive. She is also Founder and Executive Director of the FairGame Initiative, which has reached more than 1,000 students closing resource gaps in STEM education across Ohio, and serves as Vice President of the United Nations Association of Ohio. She is an EPA Award recipient, ISEF qualifier, Ohio Governor's Edison Award honoree, five-time State Science Day Superior, and National Youth Science Camp Ohio Delegate.
